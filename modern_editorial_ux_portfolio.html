How I Work

Design Principles for Quiet, Resilient Software

01

Clarity over Ornamentation

Every badge, border, and transition must justify its cognitive footprint. High-velocity operators don't want decorative flair; they want unambiguous hierarchy and effortless ergonomics.

02

Evidence-Led Iteration

I ground discovery in direct workplace observation, contextual inquiries, and task-completion telemetry—not ungrounded consensus meetings or speculative aesthetics.

03

Systemic & Tokenized Scale

Components aren't standalone drawings in Figma; they are contracts with engineering. I author semantic token hierarchies that bridge CSS, Tailwind, and React with zero drift.

Background & Craft

Eight years translating ambiguous domain problems into rigorous interfaces.

I began my career in human factors research, studying how airline pilots and ER clinicians interpret dense telemetry under fatigue. That foundational background taught me that software design is primarily an exercise in respect: respecting user attention, muscle memory, and emotional bandwidth.

Over the last six years, I have worked across San Francisco and New York, leading product design for venture-backed enterprise tooling, clinical health platforms, and algorithmic trading systems.
